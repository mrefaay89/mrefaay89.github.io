{%- comment -%} Wraps jekyll-seo-tag and fills two gaps it leaves on this blog: 1. Description. The posts open with their header image, so Jekyll's automatic excerpt is empty and every article fell back to the site description. Here a post gets the first real paragraph of its own text instead. 2. Sharing image. Without `image:` in front matter, LinkedIn and X showed no picture.
